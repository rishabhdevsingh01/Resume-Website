03 Finance & Markets

My current learning focus is on understanding businesses beyond their share prices: their financial performance, competitive position, management decisions and valuation.

Structured learning

Courses & programmes

  • CFA Program — Level I preparation: building a foundation in ethics, financial reporting, economics, investment analysis and portfolio concepts.
  • SOIC course: learning frameworks for analysing businesses, financial statements and investment opportunities.
  • Finlatics Gold — FMEP: developing practical exposure to financial markets and investment-related decision-making.
  • Red & White: Basic Accounting & Taxation learning.

Practical approach

Learning through market observation

  • Following listed companies, sectors and important market developments.
  • Practising how to read annual reports and understand key financial statements.
  • Exploring business models, competitive advantages, risks and valuation fundamentals.
  • Studying IPOs, Right issues various other corporate actions to understand a company's business, offer details and valuation before forming a view.
